June Solstice Game Jam

Solstice Cipher

Balance the longest day and the shortest night. Collect four cipher sparks, avoid the shadow drift, and open the solstice gate.

Mission

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Keep the light meter in the golden band, collect cipher sparks, then reach the gate.

Light balance
50%
Cipher
0/4
Time
90s

Press Start to begin.

Theme

The solstice is a turning point: half the world leans into light while the other half leans into dark. This game turns that into a mechanic: too much light or too much shadow destabilizes the route.

The cipher sparks are a small ode to Alan Turing: the gate only opens when you collect enough signal from the maze to decode the final path.
